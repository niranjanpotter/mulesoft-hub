⚡ Quick Answer

In 2026, all MuleSoft credentials live inside the Salesforce Trailhead certification ecosystem: the former MCD Level 1 is indexed as Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Developer (60 multiple-choice questions, 120 minutes, 70% passing score, annual Trailhead release maintenance), while niche credentials such as MuleSoft Catalyst Consultant and Hyperautomation Developer have been retired. Senior candidates target MuleSoft Integration Architect and Platform Architect alongside Agentforce + MCP hands-on skills.

Frequently Asked Questions & Interview Q&A

What happened to MuleSoft Certified Developer Level 1 (MCD Level 1) in 2026?

It is indexed under Salesforce's unified Trailhead credential naming as Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Developer, maintaining the core 60-question, 120-minute format with a 70% passing score.

Which MuleSoft certifications were retired?

Niche credentials including Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Catalyst Consultant and Salesforce Certified MuleSoft Hyperautomation Developer were phased out during Salesforce's certification consolidation.

How do you renew a MuleSoft Developer or Platform Architect certification?

Rather than retaking expensive proctored exams, Trailhead requires completing short online release maintenance modules by scheduled annual deadlines.

What is the single most-tested concept across MuleSoft Developer & Architect exams?

Error propagation across Main Flow, Private Flow, Sub-flow, and Try scopes (On-Error-Continue vs On-Error-Propagate) plus CloudHub 2.0 Private Space / RTF sizing trade-offs.

What free resources cover the 2026 certification syllabus best?

Combine hands-on XML/CLI setup guides on mulesoft.niranjanpotter.dpdns.org with the 308 scenario Q&As, DataWeave 2.0 Playground, Error Handling Simulator, and Timed Mock Interviews on niranjanpotter.github.io.
